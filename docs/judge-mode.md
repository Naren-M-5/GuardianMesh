# Judge Mode walkthrough

Judge Mode is the canonical 90-second GuardianMesh product story. It is synthetic, deterministic, local-only, and separate from the Ring Playground proof shown in the submission video.

## Customer story

A homeowner or caregiver does not need three disconnected notifications. They need to know when a quiet entrance becomes an evolving situation, why it was escalated, and what human action is appropriate.

## Demonstration beats

1. Motion at an unusual time produces a score of 27.
2. A correlated person signal raises the same situation to 46.
3. Repeated and prolonged activity raises the unified situation to 89.
4. `Why this alert?` reconstructs the score and shows counterfactuals.
5. The owner resolves the incident with a structured outcome, creating an audit record and, for benign outcomes, improving the local household baseline.

## Boundaries

- The walkthrough is clearly labeled as a simulation.
- It performs no face recognition or identity inference.
- It does not confirm an emergency or trigger autonomous emergency action.
- The submission video must separately show the Ring Playground device/API interaction.
